# Missing Data Sources & Acquisition Specification (`DATA_GAPS.md`)

**Audit Date:** 2026-11-15  
**Target Market:** Commercial Yellow Onion (`ОКПД2 01.13.43.110` / `ТН ВЭД ЕАЭС 0703 10 190 0`), EXW Volgograd Oblast (Gorodishchensky, Sredneakhtubinsky, Bykovsky, Svetloyarsky districts).

## Why Current Data Is Insufficient for Volgograd Onion Forecasting

1. **Zero Volgograd Oblast Producer Price History (`data/official-history.json`):**  
   `producer.rf` has 109 national monthly rows (`2017-01..2026-09`, missing `2021-09`), and `producer.regional` has only 106 rows across 9 federal districts/regions (`Южный федеральный округ` has 11 rows; **Volgograd Oblast has 0 rows**). National RF producer prices blend greenhouse/northern regions and retail-packaged contracts with southern field bulk sales, masking the steep Lower Volga seasonal basis swing (`~0.70x` RF in September harvest vs `~0.94x` RF in March–April).
2. **Zero Verified Local EXW Transactions (`data/observations.json`):**  
   All 16 Volgograd warehouse rows in `data/observations.json` are `indicative_offer` listings scraped or copied from AgroServer/AgroRU classifieds (`n = 0` verified closed transactions).
3. **Missing Supply-Shock Drivers (`data/macro-history.json`):**  
   While CBR exchange rates (`usdRub`, `kztRub`), CBR key rate, and Volgograd city ERA5 weather are collected, the three variables that actually cause **$\pm 30\%\text{–}60\%$ price regime shifts** in Southern Russian spring onion markets—**Central Asian import volumes & export bans**, **January–March remaining warehouse stocks**, and **spring planted area in Volgograd/Astrakhan**—are completely absent from the training data (`data/assumptions.json:14-18` sets `balance.inputs` to `null` for `production`, `stocks`, `imports`, and `exports`).
